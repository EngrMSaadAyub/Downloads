See what your agents answered, answer their permission questions, and use project buddies others share with you.

### New
- **Answers**: when a coding agent is done, its reply shows in that buddy's chat (**Show more** for long ones; hold to copy), and the notification shows its start.
- **Allow / Deny**: when an agent asks for permission, tap **Allow** or **Deny** right in the notification, or in the buddy's chat. Your PC answers it in the Claude app.
- **Shared project buddies**: type a code someone gave you under **Link a PC** to see and use the project buddy they shared. It shows as "Shared by <their email>"; **Leave** stops it.
- **Tap a notification** to open that buddy's chat.

### Notes
- Needs My PC Buddy 1.7.0 on the PC ([download](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/my-pc-buddy-v1.7.0)).
- Allow / Deny works with the Claude app for now; for Cursor and Codex, answer on the PC.

### Install (Android 8.0 or newer)
Download **PocketBuddy-1.4.0.apk** on your phone and open it. It installs over the older version and keeps your sign-in. The first time, Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser or files app: allow it, then tap **Install**.
