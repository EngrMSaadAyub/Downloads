New **AI Agents** tab: your PC's coding agents in your pocket, and a way to give them tasks.

### New
- **AI Agents tab**: link your PC once with the 6-digit code from My PC Buddy (right-click your buddy → **Coding agents → Link a phone**, then **Link a PC** here). You see your buddy and each project buddy, and whether its coding agent is working, done or needs you.
- **Notifications** when an agent is done, hits an error or needs your permission.
- **Give a buddy a task**: tap a buddy for its chat. It shows what happened lately and the tasks you sent, each with what became of it (waiting, sent, not sent). Type a task and the PC types it into that buddy's chat in its app. **To:** picks the conversation: the current one, a new one, or a recent one. Your own buddy answers here.
- **Send again**: tap a task that wasn't sent to send it again, or edit it first.
- **Locked PC**: a task waits while the PC is locked ("Waiting for you to unlock the PC") and goes once you unlock it.

### Notes
- Needs My PC Buddy 1.6.0 on the PC ([download](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/my-pc-buddy-v1.6.0)), signed in with the same email.
- The first task from this phone asks on the PC whether to allow it; the PC can turn that off any time.

### Install (Android 8.0 or newer)
Download **PocketBuddy-1.3.0.apk** on your phone and open it. It installs over the older version and keeps your sign-in. The first time, Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser or files app: allow it, then tap **Install**.
