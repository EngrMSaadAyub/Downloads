My PC Buddy's Friends chat on your phone: the same buddy, in your pocket.

### What it does
- **Same account as your PC**: sign in with the email you use in My PC Buddy's Friends window. Your friends and chats are the same on every PC and phone you're signed in on.
- **Notifications** when a message comes, with a **Reply** box right in the notification.
- **Ticks like WhatsApp**: a clock while sending, one tick on the server, two grey ticks when it reached your friend's PC or phone, two blue ticks when they've read it.
- **Retry or delete** a message that couldn't be sent (it turns red; tap and hold it).
- **Sign in with a PIN**: after your first email-code sign-in, set a 4-digit PIN and use email + PIN from then on.
- **Opens straight onto your chats**: the app shows your chats at once and syncs behind them, also when you open it from a notification.
- **Messages disappear after 24 hours**, on every device, like on the PC.

### Notes
- Works with My PC Buddy 1.5.0 on the PC ([download](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/my-pc-buddy-v1.5.0)). Your friends can use either.
- Your chats are kept on the phone encrypted (with a key that never leaves it) so the app opens instantly; signing out deletes them.

### Install (Android 8.0 or newer)
Download **PocketBuddy-1.2.1.apk** on your phone and open it. Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser or files app the first time: allow it, then tap **Install**. Sign in with the same email as on your PC.
