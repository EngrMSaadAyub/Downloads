Your coding agents on your phone: see what they're doing, and give a buddy a task from anywhere.

### New
- **Link a phone**: right-click your buddy → **Coding agents → Link a phone** shows a 6-digit code. Type it in Pocket Buddy (**AI Agents → Link a PC**). The phone then sees your buddy and every project buddy, and whether its coding agent is working, done or needs you.
- **Notifications on your phone** when an agent is done, hits an error or asks for your permission.
- **Give a task from your phone**: tap a buddy in Pocket Buddy and type. The PC types it into that project buddy's chat in its app (Claude app, Cursor or Codex), the same as typing it there. **To:** picks the conversation: the current one, a new one, or a recent one. A message to your own buddy goes to Hello Buddy, and his answer comes back to the phone.
- **You stay in charge**: the first task from a phone asks you on the PC, *"Allow <phone> to give tasks?"*. **Can give tasks** in Link a phone changes that any time, and **Remove** unlinks a phone.
- **Locked PC**: tasks wait while the PC is locked (the phone says so) and go a few seconds after you unlock it. A task not sent within 10 minutes is dropped.

### Notes
- Needs Pocket Buddy 1.3.0 on the phone ([download](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/pocket-buddy-v1.3.0)) and the Friends sign-in on the PC (same email on both).
- What goes to the phone: the buddies' names and apps, what their agents are doing, the start of the last prompt, and the titles of recent conversations. Never your files, and never what's said in the chats.
- Fixed: the Close button in Link a phone.

### Install
Download **MyPCBuddy-1.6.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `MyPCBuddy.exe` (Windows 10 1903+ / Windows 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
