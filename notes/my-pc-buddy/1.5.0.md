Ticks like WhatsApp, emoji, a sign-in PIN, and your chats on your phone too.

### New
- **Emoji**: the 😊 button in the message box opens an emoji picker (Recent, then categories). Emoji now show in colour in the chat and while you type, and a message of just one to three emoji shows them big.
- **Ticks on your messages**: a clock while sending, one tick when the server has it, two grey ticks when it reached your friend's PC or phone, two blue ticks when they've read it.
- **Retry or delete unsent messages**: a message that couldn't be sent turns red; click it for **Retry** or **Delete**. A retry never sends it twice.
- **Sign in with a PIN**: after your first email-code sign-in you can set a 4-digit PIN; from then on just type your email and the PIN. Five wrong tries lock it until you sign in with an email code once. Change it from the Friends **⋯** menu.
- **Read on one, read everywhere**: reading a chat on one PC or your phone clears the unread count on all of them.
- **On your phone**: the new **Pocket Buddy** app for Android is the same Friends chat with the same account. [Download Pocket Buddy](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/pocket-buddy-v1.2.1).

### Notes
- Your friends see ticks once they're on My PC Buddy 1.5.0 or Pocket Buddy; older versions still chat normally.
- Flags show as letters (Windows' emoji font has no flags).

### Install
Download **MyPCBuddy-1.5.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `MyPCBuddy.exe` (Windows 10 1903+ / Windows 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
