Your buddy can now talk with you, and reminders can be snoozed.

### New
- **Hello Buddy**: double-click him and press **Let's talk?** (or menu → **Talk to …**). A chat box opens, he jumps on top of it and chats with you like a friend: ask for suggestions, talk about anything, or say "remind me to stretch at 4" and he sets a real reminder. He looks down while you type, wears his thinking face while he thinks, and his face matches each answer.
- **Works with or without an AI app**: he answers through **Claude Code** or **Codex** if one is installed (your own sign-in and usage), otherwise through the free online service **Pollinations.ai** with no setup. Switch from the **via … ▾** line at the top of the chat or menu → **Talk using**. A small line under each reply shows which AI answered.
- **Nothing saved on this PC**: the chat lives only in memory. **Forget** clears it in a puff of smoke; closing the box keeps it until you quit him.
- **Minimize**: squeeze the chat down to a small bar he stands on; open it again with **^**.
- **Snooze everywhere**: a reminder now comes with **Snooze 10 min** / **Done** buttons next to him; click the Windows notification to snooze 5 / 10 / 30 / 60 minutes; or use menu → **Snooze last reminder**. Reminders that go off while you chat show up in the chat as a card.

### Notes
- Messages go to the AI service you use (Anthropic, OpenAI or Pollinations.ai). Pollinations.ai is a free third-party service: it can be slow now and then (he retries once automatically).
- Replies take a few seconds; he shows it with his thinking face and dots in the chat.

### Install
Download **MyPCBuddy-1.3.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `MyPCBuddy.exe` (Windows 10 1903+ / Windows 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
