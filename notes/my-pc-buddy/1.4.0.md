Chat with friends who have My PC Buddy too.

### New
- **Friends chat**: menu → **Friends...**. Sign in with your email and a code sent to it (no password). Add a friend by their email; once they accept, click them to chat.
- **Same chats on every PC**: sign in with the same email on your office and home PCs and carry on where you left off.
- **Messages disappear after 24 hours**, on every PC, whatever the time zone. Nothing is saved on your PC except your sign-in (encrypted for your Windows user).
- **He tells you when a message comes**: a happy note from him and a Windows notification; while messages are unread a bubble beside him says **💬 Name · 2 new**. Click either to open the chat.
- **Friends menu (⋯)**: the name your friends see, notifications on/off, sign out, delete your account. Right-click a friend to remove or block them.

### Fixed
- Clicking a reminder's Windows notification now opens the snooze choices (it did nothing before).

### Notes
- Your friend needs My PC Buddy 1.4.0 and to sign in to Friends once before you can add them.
- The sign-in email can take a minute: check your spam folder.

### Install
Download **MyPCBuddy-1.4.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `MyPCBuddy.exe` (Windows 10 1903+ / Windows 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
