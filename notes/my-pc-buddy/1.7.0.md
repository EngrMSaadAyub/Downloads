Your agents' answers on your phone, Allow / Deny from anywhere, and sharing a project buddy with someone else.

### New
- **The agent's answer on your phone**: when a coding agent is done, Pocket Buddy shows its reply in that buddy's chat, and the notification shows its start. **Coding agents → Link a phone → Send agents' answers to the phone** turns this off; then only status goes out.
- **Allow or Deny from your phone**: when an agent asks for permission, the notification has **Allow** and **Deny**. The PC answers the question in the Claude app for you, always **Allow once**, never "always allow". After Deny, the buddy is free for your next task straight away.
- **Share a project buddy**: right-click it → **Share...** shows a one-time code. Typed into someone else's Pocket Buddy, it links them to that buddy only. They see its status, notifications and answers, and can give it tasks; they never see your own buddy or other projects. Their first task asks you on the PC. The Share window lists who it's shared with, each with **Can give tasks** and **Remove**, and **Send the agent's answers to them** switches them to status only.
- **Tap a notification** to open that buddy's chat on the phone.

### Notes
- Needs Pocket Buddy 1.4.0 on the phone ([download](https://github.com/EngrMSaadAyub/Downloads/releases/tag/pocket-buddy-v1.4.0)) for answers, Allow / Deny and sharing.
- Allow / Deny works with the Claude app for now; for Cursor and Codex, answer on the PC.
- What goes to a linked phone now includes the agents' replies (up to 8,000 characters, kept 24 hours), unless you turn that off.

### Install
Download **MyPCBuddy-1.7.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `MyPCBuddy.exe` (Windows 10 1903+ / Windows 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
