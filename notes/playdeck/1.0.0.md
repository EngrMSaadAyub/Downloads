First release: a VLC-style audio and video player for your phone.

### New
- **Playlists**: tap **+** to add audio and video files from your phone (several at once). Drag the handle to change the order, swipe to remove (with **Undo**). The playlist is saved.
- **Keeps playing in the background**: music and videos carry on with the screen off, with previous / play-pause / next in the notification and on the lock screen.
- **Bluetooth and headsets**: play/pause, next/previous and volume work from your earbuds, headphones or car. Playback pauses when they disconnect.
- **Full-screen video**: swipe the left side up/down for **brightness**, the right side for **volume**. The size button switches between best fit, stretch to full screen, crop and original size. Playback speed and audio track are in the settings button.
- **Lock**: the lock button on the left blocks every control so nothing gets pressed by accident. Tap the screen to show the unlock button.
- **You choose the rotation**: videos open in portrait; the rotate button switches to landscape and back.
- **Subtitles**: load a subtitle file (.srt, .vtt, .ass, .ssa, .ttml) from your phone; it's remembered for that video. Subtitles inside the video are under the **CC** button.
- **Mini player**: menu → *When leaving the video player* → **Show mini player** keeps the video in a small floating window (with previous / play-pause / next) when you go home. Or pick **Keep playing audio only**.

### Install
Download **PlayDeck-1.0.0.apk** on your phone and open it. Allow installing from your browser or files app when Android asks, then tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details → Install anyway**. Android 8.0 or newer.
