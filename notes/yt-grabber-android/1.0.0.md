First release: YT Grabber on your phone. Paste a YouTube link, pick a quality, download.

### New
- **Every quality**: 144p up to 4K (60 fps where available), or **Audio only** as MP3. Each choice shows its approximate size.
- **Share from YouTube**: in the YouTube app tap **Share → YT Grabber** and the qualities load by themselves. Or copy a link and tap **Paste**.
- **Downloads in the background**: a notification shows progress and speed, with a **Pause** button. A second notification opens the file when it's done.
- **Pause and resume**: carries on from where it stopped, even after closing the app.
- **Saved where you expect**: **Download → YT Grabber**. Videos also show in your gallery.
- **Keeps working**: the YouTube engine (yt-dlp) updates itself once a day. Menu → **Check for update** forces it.
- Light and dark theme, following your phone.

### Which file?
- **YTGrabber-1.0.0.apk**: almost every phone (64-bit, 2017 and newer).
- **YTGrabber-1.0.0-armv7.apk**: only if the first one says it isn't compatible (older 32-bit phones).

### Install
Download the APK on your phone and open it. Allow installing from your browser or files app when Android asks, then tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details → Install anyway**. Android 8.0 or newer.

Only download videos you have the right to save. Downloading may be against YouTube's Terms of Service.
