First release: download YouTube videos in any quality, with a download manager like IDM.

### New
- **Every quality**: 144p up to 4K/8K (60 fps where available), or **Audio only** as MP3. Each choice shows its approximate size.
- **Download list**: pause, resume, queue (choose how many run at once), delete (optionally with the files), live speed, time left and progress. Categories (Video, Music), status filters (Downloading, Unfinished, Finished), search, sortable columns and a details panel with the thumbnail.
- **Add URL**: paste a YouTube link (or press Ctrl+V in the window), pick a quality card, then **Start download** or **Download later**.
- **Browser button**: the included Chrome/Edge extension lists the qualities of the video you're watching and sends your choice to YT Grabber, which comes to the front with the download.
- **Tray**: closing the window keeps YT Grabber ready in the tray. A notification shows when a download finishes. Optionally starts with Windows.
- **Keeps working**: the YouTube engine (yt-dlp) updates itself once a day. Settings also has **Check for update**.
- Light and dark theme, following Windows.

### Notes
- The browser button is a developer-mode extension (the Chrome Web Store doesn't accept YouTube downloaders): `chrome://extensions` → **Developer mode** → **Load unpacked** → the **Browser extension** folder. The app's **Extension** button walks you through it.
- 1440p and 4K come as VP9 video inside MP4. VLC and modern players handle them.
- Only download videos you have the right to save. Downloading may be against YouTube's Terms of Service.

### Install
Download **YT-Grabber-1.0.0.zip**, unzip it anywhere and run `YT Grabber.exe` (Windows 10 / 11; nothing to install). See `README.txt` inside.
